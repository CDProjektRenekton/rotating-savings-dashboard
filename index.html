          Settings are saved in this browser only. To share the rotation with everyone, add these optional columns to the Google Sheet (any order, header row anywhere in the first rows):
          • Payout Month: the month (or date) the member receives the pot, e.g. March or 3/30/2026
          • Contribution: the member's monthly share, if it differs from the usual amount
          • Received: Yes/No to confirm payouts that have been released
          A member who holds two slots (two turns) should be listed on two rows.
